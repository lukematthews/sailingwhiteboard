## Rule 14 Avoiding Contact

A boat shall avoid contact with another boat if reasonably possible.
(a) avoid contact with another boat,
(b) not cause contact between boats, and
(c) not cause contact between a boat and an object that should be avoided.
However, a right-of-way boat or one entitled to mark-room or room need not act to avoid contact until it is clear that the other boat is not keeping clear or giving room.
