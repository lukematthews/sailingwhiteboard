### 18.1 When Rule 18 Applies

(a) Rule 18 applies between boats when they are required to leave a mark on the same side and at least one of them is in the zone.

However, it does not apply

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(1) between boats on opposite tacks on a beat to windward,  

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(2) between boats on opposite tacks when the proper course at the mark for one but not both of them is to tack,

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(3) between a boat approaching a mark and one leaving it, or

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(4) if the mark is a continuing obstruction, in which case rule 19 applies.

(b) Rule 18 no longer applies between boats when mark-room has been given.

### 18.2 Giving Mark-Room

(a) When the first of two boats reaches the zone,

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(1) if the boats are overlapped, the outside boat at that moment shall give the inside boat mark-room;

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(2) if the boats are not overlapped, the boat that has not reached the zone at that moment shall give the other boat mark-room. When a boat is required to give mark-room by this rule, she shall continue to do so for as long as this rule applies, even if later an overlap is broken or a new overlap begins.

(b) Rule 18.2(a) no longer applies if the boat entitled to mark-room passes head to wind or leaves the zone.

(c) If a boat obtained an overlap inside the zone, the outside boat shall give her mark-room only if the overlap began when the inside boat was keeping clear.

(d) If there is reasonable doubt that a boat obtained or broke an overlap in time, it shall be presumed that she did not.

(e) If a boat obtained an inside overlap from clear astern or by tacking or gybing, she shall not be entitled to mark-room.

### 18.3 Tacking in the Zone

If a boat in the zone passes head to wind and is then on the same tack as a boat that is fetching the mark, rule 18.2 does not apply. The boat shall not cause the other boat to sail above close-hauled to avoid contact or prevent the other boat from passing the mark on the required side.

### 18.4 Gybing

When an inside overlapped right-of-way boat must gybe at a mark to sail her proper course, until she gybes she shall not sail farther from the mark than needed to sail that course. Rule 18.4 does not apply at a gate mark.
