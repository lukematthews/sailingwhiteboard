## Rule 20 – Room to Tack at an Obstruction

### 20.1
Hailing

When approaching an obstruction, a boat sailing close-hauled or above may hail for room to tack and avoid a boat on the same tack. However, she shall not hail unless

(a) she needs to tack soon to avoid the obstruction; and  
(b) she is sailing close-hauled or above.

### 20.2
Responding

(a) After a boat hails, the hailed boat shall respond even if the hail  
breaks rule 20.1.
(b) The hailed boat shall respond by either
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(1) tacking as soon as possible; or  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(2) immediately replying 'You tack' and then giving the  
hailing boat room to tack and avoid her.
(c) When the hailed boat responds by tacking, the hailing boat shall tack as soon as possible.

### 20.3
Passing On a Hail

If the hailed boat responds by replying 'You tack', she shall also hail the same words to any boat overlapped to windward of her. That boat shall then respond as required by rule 20.2.

### 20.4
Additional Requirements

(a) When a boat hails for room to tack, she shall give the hailed boat time to respond.
(b) From the time a boat hails until she has tacked and avoided the hailed boat, rule 18.2(a) does not apply between them.

### 20.5
Exoneration

When a boat is taking room to tack she is exonerated if she breaks a rule of Section A or rule 15 or 16.1.

### 20.6
Communications

The notice of race may specify an alternative means of communication for a boat to indicate her need for room to tack or her response, and require boats to use it.
