## Rule 19 – Room to Pass an Obstruction

### 19.1
When Rule 19 Applies

Rule 19 applies between boats when an obstruction is one they are required to leave on the same side and at least one of them is in the zone. However, it does not apply between boats on opposite tacks on a beat to windward.

### 19.2
Giving Room at an Obstruction

(a) When boats are overlapped, the outside boat shall give the inside boat room between her and the obstruction, unless she has been unable to do so from the time the overlap began.
(b) When boats are not overlapped, the boat that is clear astern shall give the boat clear ahead room to pass the obstruction.

### 19.3
Tacking at an Obstruction

While boats are passing an obstruction, a boat that is required to give room shall not cause the other boat to tack unless she has been unable to give room from the time the overlap began.
